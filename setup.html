Take it one step at a time

Turn on Google's
2-Step Verification.

Use this guide beside your own Google Account. All account changes happen on Google's website, not here.

Before you start

Use a private device you trust and have your usual sign-in method ready. Do not share your screen when passwords, setup QR codes, or recovery codes are visible.

Follow these four steps

  1. Open your Google Account

    Go directly to myaccount.google.com (opens a new tab) and sign in there. Do not follow a sign-in link from an unexpected message.

  2. Find the security settings

    Select Security & sign-in, then find How you sign in to Google.

  3. Choose 2-Step Verification

    Select Turn on 2-Step Verification. Google may ask you to verify that it is you.

  4. Follow Google's prompts and confirm

    Complete the on-screen instructions for an available method. Return to the 2-Step Verification settings and confirm it is on. Understand the method choices.

Path checked October 19, 2026: Google's official setup instructions. Screens and options can change. A work or school account may require your administrator's help.

Google may offer a passkey instead of a password-and-code flow, so not every sign-in looks the same.

Do this before you finish

Make a backup plan.

A lost phone should not leave you without a way back in. After enabling 2-Step Verification, open its settings and find Backup codes. Follow Google's options to get and print or download a set.

Keep them private, somewhere safe you can reach without that phone, such as a secure place for important documents. Each code works once. A new set replaces the old set.

Google's backup-code instructions also explain how to use an unused code through Try another way at sign-in. Backup-code downloads are unavailable in Google's Advanced Protection Program.

Ready to move on?

  • Your account settings confirm 2-Step Verification is on.
  • You know which sign-in method you set up.
  • You have a private backup method available.
