## Final prompts

### 1. Two-factor checks

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original educational website hero illustration for a beginner-friendly two-factor authentication guide. Scene/backdrop: solid near-black navy backdrop with ample clean padding, no room text needed. Subject: a simple matte laptop displaying only a row of password dots and a separate smartphone displaying one large checkmark, connected by a subtle light-blue line to a small shield with a lock. The laptop and phone are clearly separate devices, conveying password plus a second verification step. Style/medium: polished restrained editorial 3D illustration with softly rounded forms and crisp silhouettes; calm, approachable and understandable to older nontechnical adults, not a frightening hacker scene. Composition: balanced centered still life, landscape 4:3 crop, all devices fully within frame, uncluttered. Palette: near-black, slate navy, ice-blue and white with a small warm gold detail; soft studio lighting, good separation from dark backdrop. Constraints: absolutely no words, letters, numbers, brand logos, QR codes, actual verification codes, hands, people, watermarks, or extra props. This is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot. Produce one finished image.
